# GTA2 mission catalogue (all districts)

Generated by `tools/mission_catalogue.py build` from the game's own text (`data/e.gxt`, read in place), the decompiled scripts (`assets-src/missions`, see `docs/missions.md`) and the maps (phone levels); objectives and skills curated from the briefs, cross-checked with community lists. Machine-readable twin: `docs/missions-catalogue.json` (one record per job, kill frenzy and bonus level, with the script path, cars, required models, kill counters and timers, for spawning per-mission agents).

District files: `wil` = Downtown, `ste` = Residential, `bil` = Industrial. Coordinates are map blocks; `L` is the standing height z of the phone (the script's z, or for z=255 (on top) the highest floor in the map column; street = 2; L4+ = raised terrace/roof). Prerequisite respect is the `DO_PHONE_TEMPLATE` argument (green 0, yellow 2, red 4); each yellow phone pair offers a third job and each red phone a second job after the first (`follow-up`). Status is our 3D playthrough status from `docs/playthrough-downtown.md` and `docs/playthrough-districts.md` (8 Nov 2026).

## Uncertainties

- Skills/objectives are curated from the briefs and script features (`script_features` in the JSON); a job's full branch logic is only in its `.mis`. Rooftop access is set automatically when the phone stands at L4+ or a `LOCATE_*` box is at z >= 4.
- Rival-respect pairs are inferred from the template's counter arguments (consistent with pt4_wil15: a Zaibatsu job made the Loonies refuse).
- Follow-up jobs (`*m3`, `*h2`) are attached to the phones whose template names them; whether the m3 job needs both m1 and m2 first is engine logic (`DO_PHONE_TEMPLATE`), not visible in the decompile.
- SWAT/FBI/army vehicles are assumed to need a high wanted level (game behaviour, not in the job scripts).
- Bonus-level letters (A-I) and the token unlock rule come from community pages and are unverified.
- Community names differ for PC/PlayStation (e.g. WHO'S MAD? / Who's Behind You!); titles here are the PC `e.gxt` strings.
