## 3. Pedestrians

Ped sprite frames in the style: 474 (one shared body: walk, run, stand, fall, swim, burn, shoot per weapon); ped remap palettes (the ped **types**): 53. Avatars at run time: 9 Rocketbox humans, chosen by a hash of the ped object, the player always the first.

**Gap: ped type is ignored.** The game distinguishes police, SWAT, army, medics, firemen, gang members (Zaibatsu grey, Loonies green, Yakuza blue), prostitutes, Elvis impersonators and civilians only by the remap palette; the 3D people are all civilians, so a cop or a gang member is a random civilian in the ground views. The renderer already knows the sprite palette (`pal N` in its texture keys), so the fix is a palette-to-type table and per-type avatar sets (uniforms, gang colours) with the remap colour applied to the clothing material.

Animation states with no 3D equivalent (the figure keeps idling): falling/lying dead, swimming, burning, getting in or out of a car, punching, shooting, being arrested. `out/audit_ped_frames.png` shows the frame set.

## 8. Map holes (surfaces the top-down renderer never needed)

`tools/map_holes.py <game/data>` walks the three maps for cells where a street-level camera can look at a surface the map stores no face for. Two classes are real and both are now built by `src/world_mesh.cpp`:

- **lidless_ground**: a block with a ground type or road arrows but no lid, with something built over it (a road or pavement under a bridge or overhang: the deck hid it from above). Closed with a lid at the block's top, textured like the lid a neighbouring column shows at that height (`AddHiddenSurfaces`, case a). The first class of hole, `offset > 0` columns with no base, is `AddImplicitFloors` (commit 892688d).
- **deck_underside**: a lidded block over a stored empty block (GTA2 blocks have no bottom face). Closed with a downward-facing quad at the deck's base, textured with one of the deck's side faces, else its lid (case b).
- **slope_base** (a column whose first stored block is a slope) and **lidless_top** (nothing in a stack faces upward) are counted for completeness; the former are ramp foots that the ramp itself covers, the latter does not occur.

### Renderer: companion models (`dll/src/map_objects.cpp`)

A map/code-object model `<name>` may have two companions in the manifest, drawn
with the same placement after all objects:

- `<name>__lit`: drawn unlit (texture only), so a traffic-light lens, the booth's
  PHONE sign and handset, and the pickups' ground rings glow at night and stay
  bright by day;
- `<name>__glass`: drawn translucent (alpha blend, no depth writes) after every
  opaque model, so the phone and a person show through the booth's panes.

Spinning pickups (`objects_anim: spin`) are now drawn at scale 1 (authored in
blocks) and keep their authored float: fitted to each frame's sprite extent they
grew and shrank by up to 20 % as the game cycled the eight turning frames.

## 11. Lot tile family and sign plates (FIX-WORLD-9, 10 Nov 2026)

- **pmq1, bil lot seam** (`photoreal_tiles.py lotfield`). The bil car-park lots (e.g. the roof lot at x 150..158,
  y 141..148, level 3, beside the spawn) are drawn with five road-lid tiles: interior 737 and 745, kerb edges 754
  and 742, bay line 767. In the 64 px art 737 is about 8 levels darker than the others and they blend by soft
  gradients; the photo set (and the `roads/` versions of 767 and 742) kept a tone per tile, and the street views'
  tone match (`src/renderer.cpp`, one tint per tile toward the district's asphalt median) cannot make two tiles
  meet along an edge. Seen from the player's height the lot was a patchwork with hard straight edges (column x=158
  of 754/767 lighter than column 157 of 737). `lotfield` replaces the low-frequency colour of each tile's asphalt
  body (texels within 20 levels of its median luminance; gaussian 40 texels of 512, normalised over the body)
  with the tone match's median 77,80,64, keeping grain, cracks, kerb and paint; so the runtime tint is ~1 for all
  five and every edge meets. Each bay line lies inside its own 767 tile in classic too (corner to corner), so
  the line ending at a tile edge is the original layout. Regenerate:

## 13. Sign plates at street distance (FIX-WORLD-12, 10 Nov 2026)
