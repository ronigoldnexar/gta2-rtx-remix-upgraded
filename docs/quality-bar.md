# Quality bar: what a player sees and feels in the 3D views (8 Nov 2026, QUALITY-BAR)

The bar: "a GTA 6 built on GTA 2, with the feel of today's technology", and an experience that feels "fluid, fun, magical and addictive, while remaining realistic and imbued with a sense of nostalgia". Every row below compares what a current open-world game does with what our street views (third and first person) do today, with a frame or a 2-6 s clip as evidence, a severity (5 = breaks the illusion every time it happens, 1 = polish), a frequency (3 = every minute of play, 1 = rare) and the files that own the fix, so a single-objective agent can be spawned per row. The classic top-down view is out of scope (it stays GTA2's).

**What was judged.** The shared `repo/stage` (DLL of 15:53-16:32, 8 Nov), runs on the L4 build: this pass's probes `out/qb_a`, `out/qb_b` (`gamerun/qb_probe.py`, `a34_probe.py`; the host was overloaded, so `qb_a`'s view switches lagged and most of its clips stayed in the classic view, and the session was killed before `qb_a2`; the rows use the clips that did land), plus the latest frames of the other streams: `a34_after3/5` (ACTIONS-3D-4 clips), `qa_q3_*` (QA3), `actions_3d_3`, `police_3d`, `camera_polish`, `doors_dents`, `shoot_anim`, `playthrough_9` sheets. Evidence paths are under `~/gta2-linux/out/` (local, not in git); `frames/` means `out/quality_bar/frames/`. Status: `frame` = seen in a frame; `user report` = what the user saw, not re-filmed; `mechanism` = read from code/docs, no frame; `in flight` = another stream is working on it but nothing is staged or verified (VEHICLE-FEEL `dll/src/vehicle_fx.cpp`, the modern HUD `dll/src/hud3d.*`, the plain-renderer look `src/post_fx.cpp` + `dll/src/visuals.cpp`): spawn a fix agent for those only to finish and verify.

Sheet of the top 12 as frames: `out/quality_bar_sheet.png` (local). Machine-readable rows: `docs/quality-bar.json`.

Ranking score = 10 x severity + 3 x frequency (+2 for a user report).
